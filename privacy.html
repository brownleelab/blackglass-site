Blackglass

Privacy Policy

Blackglass collects no data.

The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it never connects to the internet.

Your settings (Brightness, Change Scene and Sleep Timer) and the place each scene had reached are kept on your Apple TV only. They are not sent anywhere, and they are removed when you delete the app.
