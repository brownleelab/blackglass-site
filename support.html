What it is

Blackglass is a collection of generative art for Apple TV: twelve live scenes, drawn by your Apple TV on true black, that you browse, choose and watch. There is no account and nothing to set up.

The scenes are in four families: Flow (Lava, Marble, Drift), Sky (Northern, Tree, Saturn), Travel (Skyline, Drive, Summit) and Life (Cells, Jelly, Dolphin).

Choosing a scene

Blackglass opens on its scene browser: the four families along the top, and the family's scenes as picture cards beneath. Move left or right to a card (the row goes on into the next family) and press the center of the clickpad to watch that scene full screen.

Using the remote

  • Press the center of the clickpad, or swipe or click up, to open the scene browser while a scene is playing.
  • Swipe or click left or right to step to the previous or next scene.
  • Swipe or click down to see the scene's name and the time for a moment.
  • Back closes the scene browser. With the browser closed it returns to the Home Screen.

Settings

Open the scene browser and choose the gear at the end of the bar. There are three settings; press the center of the clickpad on one to see its values, and again to choose.

  • Brightness: Dim, Standard or Bright.
  • Change Scene: Off, or move to the next scene every 15 minutes or every hour.
  • Sleep Timer: Off, 1 hour, 2 hours or 4 hours. The picture fades to black that long after you last use the remote. Press the clickpad to bring it back.

Good to know

  • While a scene is playing Blackglass keeps the display on, as a video does. To stop, press Back to leave the app, or set a Sleep Timer.
  • Blackglass makes no sound. Your music keeps playing while you watch.
  • Summit, Saturn and Drift require Apple TV 4K (3rd generation).
  • Blackglass collects no data. See the privacy policy.
