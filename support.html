What it is

Blackglass is ambient art for Apple TV: slow scenes drawn live on true black, made for a dark room. There is no account and nothing to set up.

Blackglass is an app you open from the Home Screen. It does not replace your Apple TV's built-in screen saver.

The scenes are in four families: Flow (Lava, Marble, Cells), Sky (Northern, Summit, Tree), City (Skyline, Drive) and Sea (Jelly, Dolphin).

Using the remote

  • Swipe or click left or right to change the scene.
  • Swipe or click up, or press the centre of the clickpad, to open the bar and choose a family and a scene.
  • Swipe or click down to see the scene's name and the time for a moment.
  • Play/Pause holds the scene still; press it again to carry on.
  • Back closes the bar. With the bar closed it returns to the Home Screen.

Settings

Open the bar and choose the gear at its end. There are three settings; left and right change each one.

  • Brightness: Dim, Standard or Bright.
  • Rotate: Off, or move to the next scene every 15 minutes or every hour.
  • Sleep: Off, or fade to black after 1, 2 or 4 hours without the remote being used. Any press wakes it.

Good to know

  • Blackglass keeps the screen awake while it is showing art. To let your Apple TV's own screen saver and sleep take over, set Sleep, or press Back to leave the app.
  • Blackglass makes no sound.
  • Summit requires Apple TV 4K (3rd generation).
  • Blackglass collects no data. See the privacy policy.
